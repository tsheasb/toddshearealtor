Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Todd Shea, a licensed REALTOR® with Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices California Properties ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and protects the personal information you provide through toddshearealtor.com and in the course of our real estate services.

Information We Collect

We collect information you voluntarily provide to us, including your name, email address, phone number, and any details you share about your real estate needs when you submit a form on our website, call, text, or email us. We also collect basic technical information such as your device and browser type through standard website analytics.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your inquiries, provide real estate services, send you information you have requested, and communicate with you about properties and market updates relevant to your request. We do not sell your personal information.

SMS / Text Messaging

Mobile information is never shared or sold. No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. All the categories of exclusions above regarding sharing do not include sharing data with text message originators or providers strictly for the purpose of delivering the text messages you have consented to receive.

If you provide your phone number and opt in through our website form or verbally, you consent to receive SMS text messages from Todd Shea Real Estate related to your real estate inquiry.

  • Message frequency varies based on your interaction with us.
  • Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile carrier and plan.
  • Reply HELP for assistance at any time.
  • Reply STOP to unsubscribe from text messages at any time.
  • Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of any purchase or of receiving our services.

For full SMS program terms, see our Terms of Service.

Sharing of Information

We may share your information with service providers who assist us in operating our business (such as our customer relationship management and email platforms), and as required by law. As stated above, mobile phone numbers and SMS consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services and to comply with our legal obligations. You may request that we delete your information at any time by contacting us.

Your Choices

You may opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP. You may opt out of email communications using the unsubscribe link in any email. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the details below.
