# SB Residency — Brand & Voice Reference

## Identity

Todd Shea, REALTOR® (DRE #02028163), Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices
California Properties. Serves Santa Barbara, Montecito, Carpinteria, and the
Santa Ynez Valley (also works Ventura/Ojai/Goleta but FLEX MLS territory
only covers SB County — Ventura properties need public records/portal
fallback, not FLEX MLS).

Tagline, appears at the sign-off of every issue: **"Live On Vacation | The
Vibe Is Right | Made in Santa Barbara"**

## Voice

Short, confident, salty. No corporate tone. Casual but professional — like
talking to a close friend you also respect, not ultra-casual. Todd jokes
around but stays professional; match the tone of whoever he's writing to.

**Hard rule: never use em dashes or en dashes.** Standard punctuation only
(periods, commas, colons, parentheses). Real people don't type with dashes
like that. If you catch an em dash in a draft, rewrite the sentence, don't
just swap in a comma if it changes the meaning.

### Template Lock Update: Hero and Visual Rules (October 2026 Reskin)

Replaces the hero bullet in **Section 4 (Assets)** of `sb-residency-template-lock.md`, starting with Issue 07. Everything else in the template lock stands.

#### Hero (Issue 07 onward)

- **A photo, not a graphic.** No text baked into the image. The issue title, number, and date are live HTML in Neue Haas above it.
- **Full bleed, never cropped.** It runs edge to edge on the issue page, under the title block, at the photo's own aspect ratio. The full frame always shows.
- **Source:** Todd's own photo, or one he confirms he has rights to. The issue's social post image is the default pick. Don't use listing photos as heroes; they read as an ad and need permission.
- **File:** `2026-issue-0X-<slug>-hero.jpg`, 2400px wide, JPEG quality 90, landscape.
- **Alt text:** Describe the photo plainly (example: "Palm trees and a whitewashed Spanish arcade on a downtown Santa Barbara corner"). Don't repeat the issue title.
- **Retired:** The Poppins all caps hero, the left Clay/Gold stripe, the Sky blue eyebrow, the Clay/Gold divider, and the baked-in tagline. Issues 01 to 06 keep their original heroes in the old framed layout.

#### Body photos (Issue 07 onward)

- **Every landscape photo runs full bleed**, edge to edge at its natural aspect ratio. Nothing gets cropped. That includes listing photos, follow photos, and Currently Obsessed With photos.
- **Exceptions stay in the column at natural shape:** portrait photos and square graphics. Add `-inset` to the filename (example: `2026-issue-0X-cafe-inset.jpg`). The House Radio cover is automatically inset.
- **Ask for landscape photos when possible.** Full bleed rewards wide shots at least 1600px across. Small or portrait images get the inset treatment.

#### Visual rules that apply to everything in the issue

- **Type:** Neue Haas Grotesk (Adobe Fonts kit `rta8wjb`). Mixed case, regular weight. Uppercase only on small labels. In email, the fallback stack is Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial.
- **Color restraint:** Bone and Asphalt carry the page. Clay is the only accent (links, hairline rules, focus). Gold and Shorebreak stay in the logo mark only.
- **Shape:** Square corners on every image. Hairline rules between sections. No stripes, no eyebrow stacks above headings.
- **Social graphics** for each issue should follow the same rules: the hero photo, mixed case Neue Haas, Clay as the only accent.

## Compliance

Broker attribution shortens to **"Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices"** — never
the full legal name ("...California Properties" gets dropped in body copy).

Every non-Todd listing in Window Shopping needs: agent name + office name,
MLS number, and the "not my listings" disclaimer under the section's CTA.
BHHS formal compliance review is optional per Todd's standing direction —
the safeguards above are the substitute.

## What NOT to do

- No em/en dashes, ever, anywhere in SB Residency copy.
- Don't invent stats — pull real numbers from FLEX MLS, cite the caveat if
  sample size is small (e.g., Montecito ~10 sales/month, medians swing hard).
- Don't use a third-party company's logo/trademark for "Currently Obsessed
  With" photos if it could read as an implied endorsement — Todd's own
  photo/screenshot instead.
- Don't publish sensitive personal financial/health figures even if Todd
  shares them in chat for context — build a generic version instead (see
  the Issue 02 personal dashboard mockup as the template for this pattern).
