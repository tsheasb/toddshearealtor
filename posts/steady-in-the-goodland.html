The SB Residency: Steady in the Goodland

There's something satisfying about things being dialed in, whether it's a closet or a market. Homes here aren't just selling, they're selling fast, and there are more of them moving than any other month this year. No wild swings, no headlines, just a market that's quietly humming along. Here's what the numbers actually say, plus three places worth a second look (and a little organizing inspiration at the end).

The Nitty Gritty

This issue's market, by the numbers.

Goleta, Single-Family Homes (July 2026, via FLEX MLS)

  • Median sold price: $1,080,000
  • Active median list price: $1,410,000
  • Average days on market: 12.6 days, the fastest month of the past year. June was 24.5, and the 12-month average runs closer to 30.
  • Homes sold: 27, the highest monthly total in the past 12 months.
  • Active listings: 43

The read: Goleta's moving fast and moving more. July had the quickest average days on market and the most closed sales of any month in the past year. Pricing held basically flat year-over-year once you look past a low-volume comp month, this isn't a market getting away from anyone, it's one that's just efficient right now. If you're thinking about listing in the Goodland, this is a strong window: buyers are ready, and homes aren't sitting.

Stats reflect single-family homes in Goleta only, not condos, not the wider county. We rotate through a different market each issue.

Window Shopping

Some listings that caught my eye this issue.

3260 Polo Way, Carpinteria: $4,600,000

A new build, something genuinely rare to find in Santa Barbara County. Warm, open living with those signature exposed wood beams overhead and pocketing glass doors that erase the line between indoor and outdoor. Chef's kitchen, oak floors throughout, and a backyard built for actual living, not just looking at. 4 bed | 4.5 bath | 4,072 sqft. Listing courtesy of Calcagno & Hamilton Group, Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices California Properties. (MLS #26-2897)

531 Chapala Street Unit A, Santa Barbara: $3,300,000

An extraordinary live/work condo inside a courtyard development by famed architect Jeff Shelton, the same hand behind some of downtown's most recognizable tile and ironwork. Steps from State Street, this one's less "starter unit" and more "own a piece of a landmark." 1 bed | 1.5 bath | 2,231 sqft. Listing courtesy of Jason Saltoun-Ebin. (MLS #26-2702)

3061 Foothill Road, Santa Barbara: $1,595,000

A clean, single-level ranch with a dusk-lit curb appeal that photographs like it already knows it's the one. New landscaping, updated garage, move-in ready. 3 bed | 2 bath | 1,230 sqft. Listing courtesy of Candy Hedrick, Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices California Properties. (MLS #26-2901)

Currently Obsessed With: Organization

Whatever's got my attention this cycle, tool, trend, or otherwise.

Full disclosure: this isn't really my idea. My wife works with Neat Method (@santabarbaraneat), the local organizing outfit that turns chaos into calm for a living, and she's been doing it to our house for years. I'm just finally keeping up.

Spent the last two weeks tearing into my own closet. Anything I don't wear, doesn't fit, or should've left the building years ago, gone. Turns out getting ruthless about what stays feels almost as good as buying something new.

The tool that made it stick: Essembl, an app that digitizes your closet, photo by photo, then uses AI to build outfits out of what you actually own. Once it's catalogued, it starts suggesting combinations you'd never have put together yourself.

Photo courtesy of Neat Method Santa Barbara (@santabarbaraneat).

Bigger picture: dialing in your space doesn't just make you more efficient, it clears out the noise in your head too. Less clutter in the closet, less clutter in the mind.
