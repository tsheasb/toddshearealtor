## The Nitty Gritty

*This issue's market, by the numbers.*

**Buyers are here. They're just doing the math.** Single family prices in Santa Barbara City came in about 6% under last year, and condos about 5% under. But more homes sold than a year ago, and they sold faster.

The homes that matched buyers' perceived value from day one moved in a few weeks. The ones that didn't sat. Same market, two very different outcomes.

One more thing. We count cumulative days on market, so a home that comes off and relists doesn't get a fresh clock. 15 of this window's 100 single family sales had been relisted before they sold.

**Santa Barbara City, single family homes**
*Closed sales, MLS Areas 15 (SB/East of State) and 20 (SB/West of State). Source: FLEX MLS.*

- **Jul 24 to Oct 21, 2026 (90 days):** 100 sales, median price $2,235,000, median 25.5 days on market
- **Same window in 2025 (Jul 24 to Oct 21):** 94 sales, median price $2,372,500, median 41 days on market
- **Relisted before selling:** 15 of 100

**Santa Barbara City, condos**

- **Jul 24 to Oct 21, 2026 (90 days):** 37 sales, median price $1,118,000, median 18 days on market
- **Same window in 2025:** 35 sales, median price $1,172,000, median 20 days on market

**Inventory snapshot (as of Oct 22, 2026)**

- **Single family homes:** 126 active, roughly 3.8 months of supply at the current pace
- **Condos:** 37 active, roughly 3.0 months of supply

*How I counted: closed sales in MLS Areas 15 and 20, July 24 to October 21, 2026, compared to the same dates in 2025. Days on market are cumulative, including time before any relist. Excluded: duplicate listing records, manufactured homes, and closings that appear to be off-market deals (one day on market). All figures are medians, not averages. The active counts remove two duplicate listings, one package listing that duplicates individual units, and one boat slip record. Source: FLEX MLS, deemed reliable but not guaranteed.*

**Currently Obsessed With: No Gatekeeping: Hat Brands You Don't See on Everyone**

*Whatever's got my attention this cycle, tool, trend, or otherwise.*

![Four hats from Malbon, Bare Knuckles, Eliora, and Aimé Leon Dore, lined up on a dresser](/assets/posts/2026-issue-07-hats.jpg)

This one's just for fun. I've been obsessed lately, well, maybe always, with unique hats. So no gatekeeping from me: here are some really good brands you might not see on everyone. I always get compliments when I wear these.

- **[Malbon](https://malbon.com/collections/headwear)** Husband and wife Stephen and Erica Malbon started it in Los Angeles in 2017, with a fashion-forward take on golf. It works on the course and everywhere else. Mine's the script M.
- **[Bare Knuckles](https://bareknuckles.co/collections/hats)** Started in 2017 by Jacob Keller and Cole McBride, with new collections dropping monthly. That's the Electric patch on camo in the photo, and their logo hats are the move.
- **[Eliora](https://elioraclothing.com/collections/caps)** Washed, broken-in caps in colors like aged blue and sage, the kind that look like you've owned them for years. Classic shapes, made to be worn constantly. Mine's the script E.
- **[Aimé Leon Dore](https://www.aimeleondore.com/collections/headwear-7213605867)** Teddy Santis started it in Queens in 2014. Mine has the Unisphere from the old World's Fair grounds stitched in gold. Clean, classic, and New York all over.
