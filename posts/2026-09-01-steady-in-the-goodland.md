There's something satisfying about things being dialed in, whether it's a closet or a market. Homes here aren't just selling, they're selling fast, and there are more of them moving than any other month this year. No wild swings, no headlines, just a market that's quietly humming along. Here's what the numbers actually say, plus three places worth a second look (and a little organizing inspiration at the end).

## The Nitty Gritty

*This issue's market, by the numbers.*

**Goleta, Single-Family Homes (July 2026, via FLEX MLS)**

- Median sold price: $1,080,000
- Active median list price: $1,410,000
- Average days on market: 12.6 days, the fastest month of the past year. June was 24.5, and the 12-month average runs closer to 30.
- Homes sold: 27, the highest monthly total in the past 12 months.
- Active listings: 43

The read: Goleta's moving fast and moving more. July had the quickest average days on market and the most closed sales of any month in the past year. Pricing held basically flat year-over-year once you look past a low-volume comp month, this isn't a market getting away from anyone, it's one that's just efficient right now. If you're thinking about listing in the Goodland, this is a strong window: buyers are ready, and homes aren't sitting.

*Stats reflect single-family homes in Goleta only, not condos, not the wider county. We rotate through a different market each issue.*

**Currently Obsessed With: Organization**

*Whatever's got my attention this cycle, tool, trend, or otherwise.*

Full disclosure: this isn't really my idea. My wife works with **Neat Method** ([@santabarbaraneat](https://www.instagram.com/santabarbaraneat/)), the local organizing outfit that turns chaos into calm for a living, and she's been doing it to our house for years. I'm just finally keeping up.

Spent the last two weeks tearing into my own closet. Anything I don't wear, doesn't fit, or should've left the building years ago, gone. Turns out getting ruthless about what stays feels almost as good as buying something new.

The tool that made it stick: [Essembl](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/essembl-outfit-planner/id6479642952), an app that digitizes your closet, photo by photo, then uses AI to build outfits out of what you actually own. Once it's catalogued, it starts suggesting combinations you'd never have put together yourself.
