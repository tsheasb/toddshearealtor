The SB Residency: Steady as She Goes

Santa Barbara isn't rushing anywhere, and neither should you. Here's what's actually happening in the city market, three homes worth a second look, and what's had my attention off the clock.

The Nitty Gritty

This issue's market, by the numbers.

Santa Barbara City, Single-Family Homes (August 10 to September 9, 2026, via FLEX MLS)

  • Median close price: $1,865,000, up about 5% from the prior 30 days.
  • Median days on market: 23
  • Active inventory: 217 single-family homes

Nothing dramatic here, and that's the story. Prices are ticking up a little, homes are moving at close to the same pace they were a month ago, and inventory's holding steady. No frenzy, no slowdown, just a market that knows what it's doing.

Stats reflect single-family homes in the City of Santa Barbara only, not condos, not the wider county.

Window Shopping

Some listings that caught my eye this issue.

1510 Franceschi Road: $3,595,000

Perched on the Riviera with south-facing views that run straight out to the Pacific. Vaulted ceilings, a chef's kitchen, and a private lower-level primary suite that opens right onto the gardens. 3 beds, 3 baths, 3,250 sqft on nearly half an acre. Listing courtesy of Chris Palme, Sotheby's International Realty. (MLS #26-3018)

1508 Miramar: $7,595,000

Sand-front on Miramar Beach, which is about as rare as it gets. Currently set up as a duplex, two private 1-bed/2-bath homes each with their own entrance and ocean views, with the option to convert back to a single residence. 2 beds, 4 baths, 1,550 sqft. Listing courtesy of Emily Gaul, Christie's International RE SoCal. (MLS #26-3022)

304 Sherwood Drive: $2,870,000

Tucked into a bamboo grove with koi ponds, wraparound ipe wood decking, and an imported Balinese tea house. This one's a meditation retreat that happens to have 3 beds and 2.5 baths. 2,948 sqft on 0.45 acres. Listing courtesy of Ali Evans, Berkshire Hathaway HomeServices California Properties. (MLS #26-3029)

Quick note: none of the above are my listings, just three that caught my eye this issue. Full credit to the listing agents and offices named above.

Currently Obsessed With: Camarones al Mojo de Ajo

Whatever's got my attention this cycle, tool, trend, or otherwise.

I've been on a real home cooking kick for a while now, and it's turned into one of my favorite parts of the week. There's something about standing at the stove after a full day of showings and calls that resets me. No rush, no phone, just garlic hitting hot oil.

This one's in heavy rotation lately. Garlicky, a little smoky from the dried chiles, finished with a good handful of cilantro right at the end. Pour it over Spanish rice so the rice soaks up all that sauce, and you've got a full dinner in about 25 minutes, start to finish. Every time I make this and post it, I get a couple DMs asking for the recipe, so figured I'd just make it official.

Here's the thing: most nights this beats going out. Less money, less waiting, and honestly better flavor since you control the heat and the garlic (a lot of garlic). But the real reason I keep making it isn't the shrimp. It's that cooking for my family is one of the easiest ways I know to show them I love them. Slow down, stand at the stove, put something good on the table. That's the whole game some nights.
